GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is Knowra free to read?

Yes. The goal is to make approved public articles and books available to everyone. Some books may have access restrictions if the rights holder requires them.

Can anyone publish an article?

Registered contributors can submit content. Submissions are reviewed before they appear publicly.

Can I upload any book I find?

No. Only upload books you created, have permission to distribute, or that are legally available for redistribution. Book files can be up to 5 MB (PDF or EPUB).

Does an AI review guarantee an article is correct?

No. AI can help identify issues, but it can make mistakes. Human review and reliable sources remain important.

Can I install Knowra on my phone?

Yes. Knowra works as an app. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
